= CXF interceptors, CXF features, and JAX-WS handlers

Change how SOAP requests and responses are processed on a client or a service by adding CXF interceptors, CXF features, or JAX-WS handlers, declared with annotations or in `application.properties`.

Apache CXF processes every message through a chain of interceptors, and a CXF feature packages the interceptors and settings for one capability, such as the built-in `LoggingFeature`.
JAX-WS handlers are the alternative defined by the JAX-WS specification, implemented as `SOAPHandler<SOAPMessageContext>` classes.

In every case the class is looked up as a CDI bean first, by fully qualified class name or by bean name, and instantiated through its no-argument constructor only when no bean exists, so your interceptors, features, and handlers can inject other beans.

== Where to go

* xref:user-guide/interceptors-features-handlers/cxf-interceptors-and-features.adoc[CXF Interceptors and Features] - Use them when you want the CXF processing model, including the built-in features such as `LoggingFeature` and your own interceptor classes. Interceptors and features attach to a service through the `@Features`, `@InInterceptors`, `@OutInterceptors`, `@InFaultInterceptors`, and `@OutFaultInterceptors` annotations on the service interface or implementation, or through the matching `quarkus.cxf.endpoint."/endpoint-path".*` properties (`xref:reference/extensions/quarkus-cxf.adoc#quarkus-cxf_quarkus-cxf-endpoint-endpoint-path-features[features]`, `xref:reference/extensions/quarkus-cxf.adoc#quarkus-cxf_quarkus-cxf-endpoint-endpoint-path-in-interceptors[in-interceptors]`, `xref:reference/extensions/quarkus-cxf.adoc#quarkus-cxf_quarkus-cxf-endpoint-endpoint-path-out-interceptors[out-interceptors]`, `xref:reference/extensions/quarkus-cxf.adoc#quarkus-cxf_quarkus-cxf-endpoint-endpoint-path-in-fault-interceptors[in-fault-interceptors]`, and `xref:reference/extensions/quarkus-cxf.adoc#quarkus-cxf_quarkus-cxf-endpoint-endpoint-path-out-fault-interceptors[out-fault-interceptors]`), and to a client through its client configuration.
* xref:user-guide/interceptors-features-handlers/jax-ws-handlers.adoc[JAX-WS Handlers] - Use them when you prefer the standard JAX-WS handler API to CXF-specific interceptors. Handlers attach through `xref:reference/extensions/quarkus-cxf.adoc#quarkus-cxf_quarkus-cxf-endpoint-endpoint-path-handlers[quarkus.cxf.endpoint."/endpoint-path".handlers]` and `xref:reference/extensions/quarkus-cxf.adoc#quarkus-cxf_quarkus-cxf-client-client-name-handlers[quarkus.cxf.client."client-name".handlers]`, as an alternative to the `@HandlerChain` annotation.
